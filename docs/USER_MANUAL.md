## 1. What Is Kuzmix AI?

Kuzmix AI is a **fully offline AI assistant** for Android. Everything it does happens on your device:

- **Knowledge base built in** — 2,577 concepts across science, technology, history, economics, health, and more, bundled with the app (~19.6 MB).
- **No internet required** — the app does not even request the internet permission. It works in airplane mode.
- **No accounts, no servers** — chats and settings stay in your phone's private storage.
- **Voice dictation** — speak your questions using on-device speech recognition.
- **Built-in tools** — Code Assistant, Grammar Check, Summarizer, and more.

> **Bottom line:** Kuzmix answers questions from its onboard knowledge base (definitions, explanations, comparisons, formulas, examples) — it is a study and reference companion, not a cloud chatbot like ChatGPT.

## 4. Home Screen

The Home screen is your starting point.

### Greeting header
"Good morning, [Your Name] ☀" with the tagline *"Thrive. Create. Build. Let's make it happen."* If the app is ready, an **"Local AI • Offline Ready"** badge appears below it.

### 🔎 Search bar — ask anything

The **"Search anything..."** bar searches the onboard knowledge base **as you type** (after a short pause):

1. Type a topic — e.g. `photosynthesis`, `newton laws`, `cell wall`.
2. A **Knowledge** section appears under the search bar showing matching concepts from the knowledge base, each with a short summary.
3. **Tap any result** — Kuzmix opens a **new chat and automatically asks that question for you**. The answer appears a moment later.
4. The same search also filters your **Recent Chats** by title and message text.
5. Tap the **✕** in the search bar to clear the query.

> **Tip:** If no knowledge results appear, your query may be too vague or outside the 2,577-concept library — try different wording or ask directly in chat.

### Start a new chat
The big orange **"Start a new chat"** card opens an empty conversation. Type your first message and go.

### Quick actions
Two rows of shortcut cards:

| Card | Opens |
|---|---|
| **Image Generator** | The image creation screen |
| **Code Assistant** | Coding Q&A screen |
| **Study Plan** | A new chat for planning your studies |
| **Tools** | The full toolbox |

### Recent Chats
The last five conversations (or all matching results while searching). Tap any row to reopen it. **"See all →"** opens the full Chats list.

## 5. Chatting with Kuzmix

This is the core of the app.

### Sending a message

1. Type your question in the field that says **"Message Kuzmix AI..."** (up to ~4 lines; it grows as you type).
2. Tap the orange **➤ Send** button (or use voice input — see below).
3. A **"Thinking..."** indicator appears while Kuzmix works.
4. The answer appears in a bubble on the left.

The **Send** button stays dimmed while the field is empty or while Kuzmix is thinking.

### Reading and copying messages

- **Your messages** — orange bubbles on the right.
- **Kuzmix's replies** — grey bubbles on the left with a small **K** avatar.
- Timestamps appear under each bubble.
- **Tap (or long-press) any bubble → "Copy"** to copy the full text to the clipboard. A **"Copied to clipboard"** confirmation appears at the bottom.

### Chat header

- The **conversation title** — new chats are automatically renamed after your first message (first ~40 characters of it).
- A green dot with **"Local AI"** — confirms the answer came from the on-device knowledge base.
- **⋮ menu → "Delete chat"** — removes the conversation permanently and returns you to the previous screen. *(There is no undo or confirmation, so be careful.)*

### Understanding the answers

Kuzmix answers from its **2,577-concept knowledge base**. It's great at:

- **Definitions** — *"What is photosynthesis?"*, *"What is government?"*
- **Comparisons** — *"Difference between mitosis and meiosis"*
- **Explanations** — *"Explain encryption in cybersecurity"*
- **Formulas & laws** — *"Newton's laws of motion"*
- **Study help** — *"Start studying Biology"*, *"Give me a study roadmap"*

When it can't find a good match it will honestly say something like *"I couldn't find enough information for that exact request"* instead of making something up.

> **Note:** replies are plain text — there is no markdown or code-block formatting yet. To share an answer, use **Copy**.

### Attachments
The 📎 attach button shows *"Attachments aren't available yet — Kuzmix runs fully offline for now."* Attachments are not supported in v1.0.0.

## 6. Voice Input

Instead of typing, you can **speak** your message. Everything runs on-device.

### How to use it

1. Open any chat.
2. Tap the **🎤 microphone** (between the text field and Send).
3. The first time, Android asks for the **microphone permission** — tap **Allow**.
4. The placeholder changes to orange **"Listening..."** and the mic icon turns orange.
5. Speak clearly. Recognized words are **added to the end** of anything you've already typed — they never replace your text.
6. Tap the mic again to stop (icon shows **"Stop voice input"** while active).
7. Review the text and tap **➤ Send**.

### Good to know

- Listening **pauses automatically** when you leave the app.
- A small spinner appears while your speech is being converted to text.
- Voice input works **offline** (on-device recognizer on Android 12+; on older versions it prefers the system's offline mode).
- You still tap **Send** yourself — dictation never sends a message on its own.

### Voice problems?

| Message you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| *Microphone permission denied.* | Tap Allow when prompted, or enable the mic for Kuzmix in Android Settings → Apps → Kuzmix AI → Permissions |
| *Microphone permission is permanently denied. Enable it in Settings.* | Same as above — permission must be turned on manually |
| *Offline voice input isn't available on this device.* | Your device doesn't support on-device recognition — type instead |
| *Offline speech for this language isn't installed...* | Download the offline voice pack in Android Settings, or type instead |
| *Voice recognition timed out.* | Speak sooner or tap the mic and try again |
| *No speech detected.* | Get closer to the mic and speak louder |
| *Speech recognition is busy...* | Wait a moment and tap the mic again |

### 8.1 Code Assistant

A dedicated screen for programming questions, wired to the same offline engine.

**How to use it:**

1. Open **Tools → Code Assistant**.
2. *(Optional)* Pick a language under **"Select Language"**: `Android (Kotlin)` (default), `Python`, `JavaScript`, `Java`, `HTML`, or `Other`. Selecting a language tells Kuzmix what context to assume.
3. Either:
   - **Tap a Quick Prompt** to fill the box — *Create a simple Android app*, *Fix this error in my code*, *Explain this code*, *Add a new feature*, *Generate a full project* — then edit it with your details; or
   - Type your own question in **"Ask about your code..."**
4. Tap **➤ Send**. The reply appears with an **"Analyzing..."** indicator while it works.
5. Tap any message bubble → **Copy** to copy the text.

**Good to know:**
- The conversation is saved with the title **"Code Assistant"** and appears in your Chats list.
- Responses are plain text (no syntax highlighting yet) — copy code from the bubble.
- The **"My Projects"** tab is a visual placeholder — it doesn't show anything different yet.

### 8.2 Grammar Check

An instant, fully offline writing checker.

**How to use it:**

1. Open **Tools → Grammar Check**.
2. **Paste or type your text** in the dialog box.
3. Tap **Check**.
4. Results appear as a bulleted list: `• issue — suggested fix`, or green **"No issues found."**
5. If issues were found, tap **"Copy fixed"** to copy the corrected version to your clipboard (the box itself isn't overwritten — paste it wherever you need).
6. Tap **Close** when done.

**What it catches:**

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Repeated words | "the the" → *Write it once: "the"* |
| Extra spaces | "hello   world" |
| Space before punctuation | "hello , world" |
| Missing space after a period | "end.Next" |
| Lowercase "i" | "i am" → *Capitalize it: "I"* |
| Sentence not starting with a capital | "this is..." → *Capitalize "t"* |
| Missing ending punctuation | adds a final period |
| General capitalization fixes | auto-capitalizes where needed |

> **Scope:** English only, basic mechanical rules. It does not check spelling, grammar tenses, or style.

### 8.3 Summarizer

1. Open **Tools → Summarizer**.
2. A new chat titled **"Summarizer"** opens.
3. **Paste your article or long text** into the message field and send it.

**Note:** In v1.0.0 the Summarizer is a regular chat with a preset title — there is no dedicated summarization engine. Kuzmix will respond from its knowledge base, so very long pasted articles may get a general answer rather than a true summary. Best used with *short* passages combined with a clear instruction, e.g. *"Summarize in 3 bullet points: [paste text]"*.

### 8.4 Image Generator

The image screen lets you describe and configure an image:

1. **Describe your image...** — type what you want (up to 500 characters), e.g. *"A futuristic city at night"*.
2. **Choose Style** — Realistic (default), Anime, 3D, Cartoon, Minimalist, or Artistic.
3. **Aspect Ratio** — 1:1 (default), 3:4, 9:16, or 16:9.
4. Tap **Generate**.

**Current status:** image generation needs a **local image model**, which is **not bundled with v1.0.0**. Tapping Generate shows:

> *"Local image model not installed. Import a model in Settings → AI Model to generate images offline."*

The interface is ready — once a model is added in a future update, this screen will produce images fully offline.

### 8.5 Translator & More Tools

- **Translator** → *"Translator requires a local language model. Coming soon."*
- **More Tools** → *"More tools are on the way."*

## 9. Profile & Settings

Open **Profile** from the bottom bar.

### Your card
Avatar, name, **"Free Plan"** badge, and email. Below it, three stats (**Projects 12 · Chats 48 · Tools Used 15**) — display-only placeholders in v1.0.0.

### Local AI Model status
Shows the engine state. With a standard install you'll see:

> **Knowledge Base Ready • Model Not Installed**

This is **normal** — all answers come from the built-in knowledge base. *"Local Model Ready"* appears only if a local AI model has been imported.

### Settings rows

| Row | What's inside |
|---|---|
| **Account** | Your name/email + note that details stay on-device |
| **Appearance** | **Dark / Light / System** — your choice **applies immediately** and is saved for future launches (see below) |
| **Language** | Informational — Kuzmix follows your device language (current: English) |
| **Notifications** | Tap to toggle On/Off (stored preference; no push notifications exist) |
| **Privacy & Security** | Full privacy statement (see [§12](#12-privacy--data)) |
| **Data & Storage** | Explains local storage, the 19.6 MB knowledge base, and that clearing app data removes chats |
| **AI Model** | Engine name + status; confirms *"All answers come from the local knowledge base — no cloud AI is used."* |
| **About Kuzmix AI** | v1.0.0, credits, *"2,577-concept onboard knowledge base. No internet required."* |

### Changing the theme (Appearance)

1. Profile → **Appearance**.
2. Pick **Dark**, **Light**, or **System**:
   - **Dark** — always dark theme (default)
   - **Light** — always light theme
   - **System** — follows your phone's day/night setting
3. The theme changes **instantly** (including the status bar icons). The dialog confirms: *"Your choice applies immediately and is saved for future launches."*
4. Tap **Close**.

### Log Out
The orange **Log Out** button at the bottom returns you to the welcome screen. It does **not** delete your chats or settings — you can tap *Get Started* to come right back.

## 10. What Kuzmix Can and Cannot Do

### ✅ Works today
- Answer questions from the 2,577-concept knowledge base (offline)
- Knowledge search on the Home screen with instant concept previews
- Multi-turn conversations, stored locally with timestamps
- Voice dictation (on-device)
- Copy any message
- Code Assistant with language context and quick prompts
- Offline grammar check with one-tap fixes
- Dark / Light / System themes
- Delete conversations

### 🔜 Not yet available (v1.0.0)
| Feature | What happens now |
|---|---|
| Attachments / files | Snackbar: *"Attachments aren't available yet..."* |
| Image generation | Needs a local image model (not bundled) |
| Translator | *"Coming soon"* |
| Message markdown / code blocks | Replies are plain text |
| Chat search inside Chats screen | Use the Home search bar instead |
| Renaming chats | Titles auto-set from your first message |
| "My Projects" tab | Visual placeholder |
| Real accounts / login | "Log In" behaves like "Get Started" |
| Internet / cloud AI | Never — by design |

### Things to keep in mind
- **Ask in the knowledge base's wheelhouse** — school subjects, science, tech, history, economics, health. Creative writing, live news, and personal opinions are outside its scope.
- **No undo for deletions** — *Delete chat* removes the conversation immediately.
- **Honest fallbacks** — *"I couldn't find enough information..."* means the topic isn't in the library, not that the app is broken.

## 11. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| App opens to a spinner | Normal — settings are loading. If it lasts long, force-close and reopen |
| Welcome screen appears again | You tapped **Log Out**. Tap **Get Started** — chats are untouched |
| No answer / *"couldn't find enough information"* | Rephrase with key terms from the topic (e.g. use the concept's exact name) |
| Voice mic does nothing | Check the microphone permission (see [§6](#6-voice-input)) |
| *"Offline voice input isn't available"* | Device lacks on-device recognition — type instead |
| Theme not changing | Profile → Appearance → tap Dark/Light/System again; it applies instantly |
| *"Local image model not installed"* | Expected in v1.0.0 — see [§8.4](#84-image-generator) |
| Attachments not working | Expected in v1.0.0 |
| Want a fresh start | Delete individual chats via **⋮ → Delete chat**, or clear app data in Android Settings → Apps → Kuzmix AI → Storage (removes **all** chats and settings) |
| Answer text needed elsewhere | Tap the bubble → **Copy** |

## 12. Privacy & Data

Kuzmix AI's privacy stance, straight from the app:

> *"Kuzmix AI works completely offline. No chats, voice recordings, or personal data ever leave your device. There are no accounts on any server and the app never connects to the internet."*

Key facts:

- **No internet permission** — the app is technically incapable of going online.
- **No telemetry, no analytics, no ads.**
- **Conversations** are stored in the app's private database on your phone.
- **Voice audio** is processed on-device by your phone's speech recognizer and is never uploaded.
- **Knowledge base** (19.6 MB) ships inside the app.
- **Removing the app or clearing app data** deletes all chats permanently.
